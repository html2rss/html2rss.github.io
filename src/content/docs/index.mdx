`html2rss` turns websites into stable RSS and JSON feeds. The recommended path is **html2rss-web**: paste a page URL, enter an access token, and subscribe to the generated feed. Auto-source (with the Botasaurus companion for JavaScript-heavy sites) handles extraction without hand-written selectors.

## Typical uses

- Follow blogs and news without social algorithms.
- Track product changelogs and release notes.
- Monitor job boards and community forums.
- Subscribe when a site does not publish its own feed.
